02 / CONTEXT

Your closet is part of a bigger story.

Clothing waste is a systems problem, not just an individual shopping choice. The EPA estimated about 13 million US short tons of clothing and footwear waste in 2018.

Source: US EPA, Clothing and Footwear (2018). This national waste estimate cannot describe the shopping habits of NYC college students.
